How to Play

1. Swipe Swipe in one direction. Every tile moves as far as it can.
2. Merge Two matching tiles that collide become the next character.
3. One merge A tile can merge once per swipe, so 2 + 2 + 2 becomes 4 + 2.
4. New tile After a valid move, a new Endo Bolt or Pizza Slice appears.

Reach 2048 Midnight Show. The game ends when the board is full and no neighboring tiles match.
